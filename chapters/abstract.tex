\begin{abstract}{家庭服务机器人，机械臂操作, 模仿学习, 扩散模型，视觉语言动作模型}
随着人工智能与机器人技术的发展，机械臂自主操作场景逐渐拓展至家庭服务等非结构化场景。视觉语言动作模型（Visual-Language Action Model，VLA）通过统一视觉语言感知与理解，为实现基于自然语言指令的机器人自主操作提供了重要路径。然而，现有视觉语言动作模型在家庭场景中的操作任务中仍面临两方面挑战：一方面，传统动作学习方法难以有效刻画真实机器人操作中的多模态分布；另一方面，现有方法缺乏对历史上下文的有效维护，容易在长时序任务中出现状态混淆与上下文遗忘。针对上述问题，本文提出了一种基于特征时序缓存的视觉语言动作模型。主要研究工作如下：

（1）针对机械臂自主操作任务中控制执行与数据采集缺乏统一流程的问题，研究了机械臂运动学建模，自主控制流程与真实操作数据构建方法。本文基于D-H参数法建立了机械臂运动学模型，构建了统一动作表示，并梳理了机械臂自主操作控制执行的闭环流程。构建了基于遥操作的真实数据采集流程，并按照RLDS规范组织操作数据集，为后续模型训练，动作转换和真实平台验证提供了支撑。

（2）针对传统方法在精细操作任务执行中存在不足的问题，提出了一种基于扩散模型的动作生成算法。将动作序列生成视为条件去噪过程，通过扩散模型学习多种可行动作分布，并结合动作分块与加速采样提升推理效率。在 RoboMimic 数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在 Transport ph 和 Tool Hang ph 任务上分别取得了0.862和0.791的成功率，说明该方法能够有效地学习多模态动作分布。

（3）针对家庭环境中长时序操作任务的状态混淆和上下文遗忘的问题，提出了一种基于特征时序缓存的视觉语言动作模型TemporalCacheVLA。通过感知语义双通道缓存机制保存历史视觉与任务语义信息，并将历史上下文融入当前动作生成过程，从而增强模型对长时序任务的理解能力。在 LIBERO 基准数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在 Goal 与 LIBERO-100 子集上分别取得了0.959和0.949的平均成功率，消融实验进一步说明了时序缓存机制的有效性。

（4）针对视觉语言动作模型在真实家庭场景中部署验证时面临的数据分布差异和执行稳定性问题，开展了真实场景数据采集与机械臂自主操作实验。本文在真实桌面场景中采集简单抓取，抽屉操作和长时序组合任务数据，并利用自建数据集对TemporalCacheVLA进行参数冻结微调。真实机械臂实验结果表明，TemporalCacheVLA在三类任务中分别取得了0.85，0.65和0.40的成功率，平均成功率达到0.633，高于OpenVLA基线模型的0.417。结果说明，本文方法在真实家庭场景机械臂操作任务中具有一定部署可行性，并在长时序组合任务中表现出更明显的优势。

\end{abstract}

\begin{englishabstract}{Home service robots, robot manipulation, imitation learning, diffusion models, visual-language-action models}
With the development of artificial intelligence and robotics, autonomous robot manipulation has gradually extended to unstructured scenarios such as home service environments. Visual-language-action models (VLA) provide an important pathway for robot autonomous manipulation based on natural language instructions by unifying visual-language perception and understanding. However, existing VLA methods still face two major challenges in long-horizon and unstructured manipulation tasks. On the one hand, conventional action learning methods struggle to effectively characterize the multimodal action distributions in real-world robotic manipulation. On the other hand, existing methods lack effective maintenance of historical context, which may lead to state confusion and context forgetting in long-horizon tasks. To address these problems, this thesis proposes a visual-language-action model based on temporal feature cache. The main contributions are summarized as follows.

(1) To address the lack of a unified process for control execution and data collection in autonomous robotic arm manipulation, this thesis studies robotic arm kinematic modeling, autonomous control processes, and real-world manipulation dataset construction. A kinematic model of the robotic arm is established based on the D-H parameter method, a unified action representation is constructed, and the closed-loop control execution process for autonomous robotic arm manipulation is formulated. A teleoperation-based real-world data collection process is further developed, and the manipulation dataset is organized according to the RLDS format, providing support for subsequent model training, action transformation, and real-world platform validation.

(2) To address the limitations of traditional methods in fine-grained manipulation tasks, this thesis proposes an action generation algorithm based on diffusion denoising. Continuous action sequence generation is formulated as a conditional denoising process, in which a diffusion model is used to learn diverse feasible action distributions. Action chunking and accelerated sampling are further incorporated to improve inference efficiency. Experiments on the RoboMimic dataset show that the proposed method achieves success rates of 0.862 and 0.791 on the Transport ph and Tool Hang ph tasks, respectively, demonstrating its ability to effectively learn multimodal action distributions.

(3) To address state confusion and context forgetting in long-horizon manipulation tasks in home environments, this thesis proposes TemporalCacheVLA, a visual-language-action model based on temporal feature cache. A dual-channel cache mechanism is designed to preserve historical visual information and task semantic information, and historical context is incorporated into the current action generation process, thereby enhancing the model's ability to capture state changes in multi-stage tasks. Experiments on the LIBERO benchmark show that the proposed method achieves average success rates of 0.959 and 0.949 on the Goal and LIBERO-100 subsets, respectively. Ablation experiments further verify the effectiveness of the temporal cache mechanism.

(4) To address the data distribution gap and execution stability problems encountered when deploying visual-language-action models in real-world home scenarios, this thesis conducts real-world data collection and autonomous robotic arm manipulation experiments. Data are collected in real desktop scenarios for simple grasping, drawer manipulation, and long-horizon compositional tasks. TemporalCacheVLA is then fine-tuned on the self-built dataset with parameters frozen. Real-world robotic arm experiments show that TemporalCacheVLA achieves success rates of 0.85, 0.65, and 0.40 on the three types of tasks, respectively, with an average success rate of 0.633, outperforming the OpenVLA baseline model with an average success rate of 0.417. These results indicate that the proposed method has practical feasibility for robotic arm manipulation in real-world home scenarios and shows more significant advantages in long-horizon compositional tasks.
\end{englishabstract}
