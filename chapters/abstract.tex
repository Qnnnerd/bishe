\begin{abstract}{机械臂操作, 模仿学习, 扩散模型, 视觉语言动作模型, 时序缓存}
随着人工智能与机器人技术的不断发展，具身智能正成为科技发展的重点领域。然而，真实世界非结构化环境中的复杂因素以及长视野任务中的时序上下文依赖，严重影响了机械臂在复杂操作任务中的成功率。因此，本文针对多模态动作生成与决策依赖难题，提出了一种基于特征时序缓存的视觉语言动作模型TemporalCacheVLA。本文的主要研究工作如下：

（1）针对机械臂运动映射问题，研究了基于运动学理论与典型模仿学习算法的机器人操作基础理论。采用Denavit-Hartenberg参数法对Franka Panda与UR5e进行运动学建模，推导正逆运动学方程，基于自建数据集计算真实场景动作标签，为后续算法提供了正运动学支撑。

（2）针对传统行为克隆难以拟合多模态动作分布的问题，提出了一种基于扩散模型的模仿学习动作生成算法。采用迭代DiT（Diffusion Transformer， DiT）为核心，利用EfficientViT提取视觉特征，并设计非对称跨模态注意力机制融合多模态特征，采用去噪扩散隐式模型（Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models， DDIM）加速采样。在RoboMimic数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在Transport ph和Tool Hang ph任务上分别将成功率提升至$0.862$和$0.791$，在Transport mh任务上由$0.643$提高至$0.801$，有效平衡了生成质量与推理效率。

（3）针对长时序任务中的状态混淆与上下文遗忘问题，提出了一种基于特征时序缓存的视觉语言动作模型TemporalCacheVLA。采用双分支视觉编码器与LLaMA 2 大语言模型提取特征，设计双通道特征时序缓存机制，通过交叉注意力检索与可学习门控动态融合历史上下文，并引入余弦相似度缓存压缩策略。在LIBERO基准数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在Goal与LIBERO-100子集上分别取得$95.9$和$94.9$的平均成功率，消融实验进一步验证了缓存检索与门控融合等机制的有效性。

（4）针对模型向真实物理世界迁移面临的系统集成难题，搭建了基于GELLO遥操臂的真实物理平台并完成部署验证。利用缩比遥操臂采集示教数据进行参数冻结微调，基于ROS框架开发本地控制节点并部署为Flask远端服务。实机评估表明，TemporalCacheVLA在简单抓取、抽屉操作及长时效组合任务中分别取得$85$、$65$和$40$的成功率，其中长时效组合任务较基线提升了$30$个百分点，证明了所提框架在真实环境中的可行性。

\end{abstract}

\begin{englishabstract}{Robotic Manipulation, Imitation Learning, Diffusion Model, Vision-Language-Action Model, Temporal Cache}
With the continuous development of artificial intelligence and robotics technology, embodied intelligence has become a key area of scientific and technological development. However, complex factors in unstructured real-world environments and the temporal context dependency in long-horizon tasks severely affect the success rate of robotic manipulators in complex manipulation tasks. Therefore, aiming at the challenges of multi-modal action generation and decision dependency, this thesis proposes TemporalCacheVLA, a vision-language-action model based on feature temporal caching. The main research work of this thesis is as follows:

(1) Aiming at the motion mapping problem of robotic manipulators, the fundamental theory of robot manipulation based on kinematics and typical imitation learning algorithms is studied. The Denavit-Hartenberg parameter method is adopted to model the kinematics of Franka Panda and UR5e, and the forward and inverse kinematics equations are derived. Action labels for real-world scenarios are calculated based on a self-built dataset, providing forward kinematics support for subsequent algorithms.

(2) Aiming at the problem that traditional behavioral cloning is difficult to effectively fit multi-modal action distributions, an imitation learning action generation algorithm based on diffusion models is proposed. Iterative Diffusion Transformer (DiT) is adopted as the core, EfficientViT is utilized to extract visual features, an asymmetric cross-modal attention mechanism is designed to fuse multi-modal features, and Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models (DDIM) is adopted to accelerate sampling. Experiments on the RoboMimic dataset show that the proposed method improves the success rates on Transport ph and Tool Hang ph tasks to $0.862$ and $0.791$, respectively, and increases the success rate on Transport mh task from $0.643$ to $0.801$, effectively balancing generation quality and inference efficiency.

(3) Aiming at the problems of state confusion and context forgetting caused by single-frame decision-making in long-horizon tasks, TemporalCacheVLA, a vision-language-action model based on feature temporal caching, is proposed. A dual-branch visual encoder and the LLaMA 2 large language model are adopted to extract features, and a dual-channel feature temporal caching mechanism is designed. Historical context is dynamically fused through cross-attention retrieval and learnable gated fusion, and a cosine similarity-based cache compression strategy is introduced. Experiments on the LIBERO benchmark dataset show that the proposed method achieves average success rates of $95.9$ and $94.9$ on the Goal and LIBERO-100 subsets, respectively. Ablation studies further verify the effectiveness of mechanisms such as cache retrieval and gated fusion.

(4) Aiming at the system integration challenges faced when migrating models to the real physical world, a real physical platform based on the GELLO teleoperation arm is built, and deployment verification is completed. A scaled teleoperation arm is utilized to collect demonstration data for parameter-frozen fine-tuning. A local control node is developed based on the ROS framework and deployed as a Flask remote service. Real-world evaluations show that TemporalCacheVLA achieves success rates of $85$, $65$, and $40$ in simple grasping, drawer manipulation, and long-horizon combination tasks, respectively. Among them, the success rate of the long-horizon combination task is $30$ percentage points higher than the baseline, proving the feasibility of the proposed framework in real environments.
\end{englishabstract}
