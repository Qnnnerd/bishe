\begin{abstract}{机械臂操作, 模仿学习, 扩散模型, 视觉语言动作模型, 时序缓存}
随着人工智能与机器人技术的不断发展，具身智能正成为科技发展的重点方向。视觉语言动作模型（Visual-Language Action Model，VLA）通过统一融合视觉感知、语言理解与动作生成，为实现基于自然语言指令的机器人自主操作提供了重要路径。然而，现有视觉语言动作模型在长时序、非结构化操作任务中仍面临两方面挑战：一方面，传统单峰动作学习方法难以有效刻画真实机器人操作中的多模态动作分布；另一方面，现有方法缺乏对历史上下文的有效维护，容易在多阶段任务中出现状态混淆与上下文遗忘。针对上述问题，本文提出了一种基于特征时序缓存的视觉语言动作模型 TemporalCacheVLA。本文的主要研究工作如下：

（1）针对模型训练与真实部署中涉及的动作表示与控制映射问题，研究了基于运动学理论与典型模仿学习算法的机器人操作基础理论。采用 Denavit-Hartenberg 参数法对 Franka Panda 与 UR5e 进行运动学分析，推导正向与逆向运动学方程，并统一采用七维末端增量动作表示构建真实场景动作标签，为后续算法设计、数据集构建及真机执行提供理论支撑。

（2）针对传统行为克隆难以有效表征多模态动作分布的问题，提出了一种基于扩散模型的模仿学习动作生成算法。在 RoboMimic 数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在 Transport ph 和 Tool Hang ph 任务上分别取得了 $0.862$ 和 $0.791$ 的成功率，在 Transport mh 任务上由 $0.643$ 提高至 $0.801$，说明该方法能够较有效地学习复杂多模态动作分布，并在生成质量与推理效率之间取得较好的平衡。

（3）针对长时序任务中的状态混淆与上下文遗忘问题，提出了一种视觉语言动作模型 TemporalCacheVLA，其核心在于双通道特征时序缓存机制。该模型通过双通道历史存储、缓存检索、门控融合与压缩更新策略，实现对长时序上下文的高效维护与动态利用。在 LIBERO 基准数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在 Goal 与 LIBERO-100 子集上分别取得了 $0.959$ 和 $0.949$ 的平均成功率，消融实验进一步说明了时序缓存机制的有效性。

（4）针对视觉语言动作模型向真实物理世界迁移部署时面临的数据采集与系统集成难题，搭建了基于 GELLO 遥操臂的真实物理平台，并完成了数据采集、参数冻结微调、远端推理服务部署与基于 ROS 的本地控制系统集成。在真实机械臂平台上的评估结果表明，TemporalCacheVLA 在简单抓取、抽屉操作及长时序组合任务中分别取得了 $0.85$、$0.65$ 和 $0.40$ 的成功率，其中长时序组合任务较基线模型提升了 $0.30$。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}{robot manipulation, imitation learning, diffusion model, vision-language-action model, temporal cache}
With the continuous development of artificial intelligence and robotics, embodied intelligence has become an important direction of scientific and technological advancement. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models provide a promising paradigm for robot autonomy under natural language instructions by unifying visual perception, language understanding, and action generation. However, existing VLA models still face two major challenges in long-horizon and unstructured manipulation tasks. On the one hand, conventional unimodal action learning methods are insufficient for accurately modeling the multimodal action distributions inherent in real-world robot manipulation. On the other hand, current methods lack effective mechanisms for maintaining historical context, which often leads to state ambiguity and contextual forgetting in multi-stage tasks. To address these issues, this thesis proposes a feature temporal cache based vision-language-action model, termed TemporalCacheVLA. The main contributions of this thesis are as follows:

(1) To address the problems of action representation and control mapping involved in both model training and real-world deployment, the fundamental theories of robot manipulation based on kinematics and representative imitation learning methods are systematically studied. Denavit-Hartenberg parameterization is adopted to analyze the kinematics of the Franka Panda and UR5e manipulators, and the corresponding forward and inverse kinematics are derived. A unified seven-dimensional end-effector delta action representation is further employed to construct action labels for real-world scenarios, providing theoretical support for subsequent algorithm design, dataset construction, and real-robot execution.

(2) To overcome the limitation of traditional behavior cloning in modeling multimodal action distributions, a diffusion model based imitation learning algorithm for action generation is proposed. Experimental results on the RoboMimic dataset show that the proposed method achieves success rates of $0.862$ and $0.791$ on the Transport ph and Tool Hang ph tasks, respectively, and improves the success rate on the Transport mh task from $0.643$ to $0.801$. These results demonstrate that the proposed method can effectively learn complex multimodal action distributions while achieving a favorable balance between generation quality and inference efficiency.

(3) To mitigate state ambiguity and contextual forgetting in long-horizon tasks, a vision-language-action model named TemporalCacheVLA is proposed, whose core lies in a dual-channel feature temporal cache mechanism. Through dual-channel historical storage, cache retrieval, gated fusion, and compressed update strategies, the model is able to efficiently maintain and dynamically exploit long-term temporal context. Experimental results on the LIBERO benchmark show that the proposed method achieves average success rates of $0.959$ and $0.949$ on the Goal and LIBERO-100 subsets, respectively. Ablation studies further verify the effectiveness of the temporal cache mechanism.

(4) To address the challenges of data collection and system integration in transferring and deploying vision-language-action models to the real physical world, a real-world robotic platform based on a GELLO teleoperation arm is constructed. A complete pipeline is established, including data collection, parameter-frozen fine-tuning, remote inference service deployment, and integration with a ROS-based local control system. Experimental results on a real robotic platform show that TemporalCacheVLA achieves success rates of $0.85$, $0.65$, and $0.40$ on simple grasping, drawer manipulation, and long-horizon composite tasks, respectively, with an improvement of $0.30$ over the baseline model on the long-horizon composite task.

\end{abstract}
