\begin{abstract}{视觉语言动作模型，机械臂操作，模仿学习，扩散模型，家庭服务机器人}
随着人工智能与机器人技术的发展，机械臂自主操作场景逐渐拓展至家庭服务等非结构化场景。视觉语言动作模型（Vision-Language-Action Model，VLA）通过统一视觉语言感知与理解，为实现基于自然语言指令的机器人自主操作提供了重要路径。然而，现有视觉语言动作模型在家庭场景中的操作任务中仍面临两方面挑战：一方面，传统动作学习方法难以实现精细化操作，容易导致末端操作过程不连续；另一方面，现有方法缺乏对历史上下文的有效维护，容易在长时序任务中出现状态混淆与上下文遗忘。因此，围绕家庭服务等非结构化场景中机械臂精细化操作能力不足与长时序上下文维护困难等问题，研究基于特征时序缓存视觉语言动作模型的机械臂自主操作技术，对于提升机械臂在真实环境中的动作稳定性、任务连续性与自主操作能力具有重要意义。主要研究工作如下：

（1）研究了机械臂运动学建模，自主控制流程与真实操作数据构建方法。本文基于D-H参数法建立了机械臂运动学模型，构建了统一动作表示，并梳理了机械臂自主操作控制执行的闭环流程。构建了基于遥操作的数据采集流程，为后续模型训练和真实平台验证提供了支撑。

（2）针对传统方法在精细操作任务执行中存在不足的问题，提出了一种基于扩散模型的动作生成算法。通过融合多模态特征输入进扩散模型学习多种可行动作分布，并提升机械臂操作的精细程度。在 RoboMimic 数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在运输任务和工具悬挂任务上分别取得了0.862和0.791的成功率,高于LSTM-GMM基线模型的0.656和0.460，说明扩散式动作生成方法能够提升复杂操作任务中的动作生成质量。

（3）针对家庭环境中机械臂长时序操作任务的状态混淆和上下文遗忘的问题，提出了一种基于特征时序缓存的视觉语言动作模型TemporalCacheVLA。通过感知语义双通道缓存机制保存历史视觉与任务语义信息，并将历史上下文融入当前动作生成过程，从而增强模型对长时序任务的理解能力。在 LIBERO 基准数据集上的实验表明，本文方法在 Goal 与 LIBERO-100 子集上分别取得了0.959和0.949的平均成功率，高于OpenVLA基线模型的0.792和0.735，验证了特征时序缓存机制在多阶段操作任务中的有效性。

（4）开展了真实场景数据采集与机械臂自主操作实验。本文在真实桌面场景中采集简单抓取，抽屉操作和长时序组合任务数据，并利用自建数据集对TemporalCacheVLA进行参数冻结微调。真实机械臂实验结果表明，TemporalCacheVLA在三类任务中分别取得了0.85，0.65和0.40的成功率，平均成功率达到0.633，高于OpenVLA基线模型的0.417。结果说明，本文方法在真实家庭场景机械臂操作任务中具有一定部署可行性，并在长时序组合任务中表现出更明显的优势。

\end{abstract}

\begin{englishabstract}{Vision-Language-Action models, robot manipulation, imitation learning, diffusion models, home service robots}
With the development of artificial intelligence and robotics, autonomous robot manipulation has gradually expanded to unstructured scenarios such as home service environments. Vision-Language-Action models (VLA) provide an important pathway for robot autonomous manipulation based on natural language instructions by unifying vision-language perception and understanding. However, existing VLA models still face two major challenges in manipulation tasks within home scenarios. On the one hand, conventional action learning methods struggle to achieve fine-grained manipulation, which may lead to discontinuous end-effector operation processes. On the other hand, existing methods lack effective maintenance of historical context, which may lead to state confusion and context forgetting in long-horizon tasks. Therefore, focusing on the insufficient fine-grained manipulation capability and the difficulty of maintaining long-horizon context in unstructured scenarios such as home service environments, it is of great significance to study robot manipulation technology based on a Vision-Language-Action model with temporal feature cache for improving motion stability, task continuity, and autonomous manipulation capability in real-world environments. The main research work is summarized as follows.

(1) Robot kinematic modeling, autonomous control processes, and real-world manipulation data construction methods are studied. A kinematic model of the robot is established based on the D-H parameter method, a unified action representation is constructed, and the closed-loop control execution process for autonomous robot manipulation is organized. A teleoperation-based data collection process is also developed, providing support for subsequent model training and real-world platform validation.

(2) To address the limitations of traditional methods in fine-grained manipulation tasks, this thesis proposes an action generation algorithm based on diffusion models. By incorporating multimodal feature inputs, the diffusion model learns diverse feasible action distributions and improves the fine-grained manipulation capability of robot manipulation. Experiments on the RoboMimic dataset show that the proposed method achieves success rates of 0.862 and 0.791 on the transport task and the tool hanging task, respectively, outperforming the LSTM-GMM baseline model with success rates of 0.656 and 0.460. These results indicate that diffusion-based action generation can improve the quality of action generation in complex manipulation tasks.

(3) To address state confusion and context forgetting in long-horizon manipulation tasks in home environments, this thesis proposes TemporalCacheVLA, a Vision-Language-Action model based on temporal feature cache. A dual-channel cache mechanism for perception and semantics is used to preserve historical visual information and task semantic information, and historical context is incorporated into the current action generation process, thereby enhancing the model's understanding ability in long-horizon tasks. Experiments on the LIBERO benchmark show that the proposed method achieves average success rates of 0.959 and 0.949 on the Goal and LIBERO-100 subsets, respectively, outperforming the OpenVLA baseline model with success rates of 0.792 and 0.735. The results verify the effectiveness of the temporal feature cache mechanism in multi-stage manipulation tasks.

(4) Real-world data collection and autonomous robot manipulation experiments are conducted. Data are collected in real desktop scenarios for simple grasping, drawer manipulation, and long-horizon compositional tasks, and TemporalCacheVLA is fine-tuned on the self-built dataset with frozen parameters. Real-world robot experiments show that TemporalCacheVLA achieves success rates of 0.85, 0.65, and 0.40 on the three types of tasks, respectively, with an average success rate of 0.633, outperforming the OpenVLA baseline model with an average success rate of 0.417. These results indicate that the proposed method has certain deployment feasibility for robot manipulation tasks in real-world home scenarios and shows more evident advantages in long-horizon compositional tasks.
\end{englishabstract}
